# BRICK #2 — PCT7: P(forward 5-day return ≥ +7%)

**Validated 2026-09-05.** Second validated signal in this fund, after the
short-interest brick. Reproducible via `analysis/pct7_gauntlet.py` and
`analysis/pct7_status.py`.

**Operating threshold: 0.30.** Not the monitor's 0.20 default — that single
choice is the difference between −0.05pp and +1.13pp of alpha, and the reasoning
is in §3.

---

## What it is

A classifier predicting the probability that a stock returns **≥ +7% over the
next 5 trading days**. Distinct from the direction model, which predicts sign.
This predicts a *large move*.

Trained **2026-05-25**, wired into `signals/generator.py` in shadow mode —
logged to `predictions.prob_pct7`, never traded. The implementation plan said
*"log alongside production for 1-2 weeks before promoting."*

**It ran fourteen weeks and nobody scored it.** 24,741 predictions accumulated
unread, on a model that was never retrained. That neglect is the reason the
evidence is strong: **the entire record is out-of-sample on a frozen model.** No
refit, no tuning, no look-ahead — the cleanest test this project has produced,
by accident.

---

## The gauntlet

Same tests the SI brick passed, and the same tests that killed insider selling
across six constructions, rate beta, IV skew conditioning, and the selective
classifier earlier in the same session.

### 1. Per-date lift with Newey-West

3,506 stock-days are **not** 3,506 observations — a market-wide move correlates
every name on a date. Treating pooled rows as independent has inflated
t-statistics 10–20× in this project before. The unit is the DATE, so the
effective sample is 58–65, not thousands.

| threshold | dates | mean lift | NW t | null t | positive dates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.20 | 65 | +8.68pp | **+4.17** | −1.32 | 48/65 |
| **0.30** | 58 | +8.83pp | **+3.34** | +1.37 | 40/58 |

Both clear |t| ≥ 3.0 with the null under 1.5.

### 2. By month — no concentration

| month | 0.20 | 0.30 |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-05 | +11.8pp | +2.8pp |
| 2026-06 | +7.2pp | +5.6pp |
| 2026-07 | +8.4pp | +14.3pp |
| 2026-08 | +9.9pp | +7.9pp |

Positive every month at both thresholds. A false positive concentrates in one
period; this does not.

### 3. Economic value — where the threshold decides everything

Hit rate is not money. A +7% target implies wide dispersion, so the losing tail
matters as much as the wins.

| | threshold 0.20 | threshold 0.30 |
|---|---|---|
| selected | 3,506 | 1,105 |
| mean return | **+0.29%** | **+1.46%** |
| median | −0.66% | +0.85% |
| universe mean | +0.34% | +0.34% |
| **vs universe** | **−0.05pp** | **+1.13pp** |
| hits | 800 @ +16.11% | 320 @ +17.07% |
| misses | 2,706 @ −4.39% | 785 @ −4.90% |
| net at 20bps | +0.09% | **+1.26%** |
| net at 40bps | −0.11% | **+1.06%** |

**At 0.20 the signal is real and worthless.** NW t = +4.17 with a clean null and
48/65 positive dates — genuinely predictive — and yet the selected names return
+0.29% against a universe mean of +0.34%. It finds names that hit +7% more often
AND lose more often, netting to slightly worse than doing nothing.

**At 0.30 it is real and profitable.** +1.13pp over the universe, surviving
40bps of cost. The median flips from −0.66% to +0.85%, which is the clearest
single indicator that the 0.30 cut is selecting a different population rather
than just fewer names.

**This is why hit-rate lift alone is not a verdict.** Both thresholds show ~+8.7pp
of lift and clean statistics. Only one makes money.

### 4. Regime dependence — the honest caveat

Bucketed by the universe's own base rate that day, a proxy for how many large
moves were available at all:

| regime | dates | lift @0.20 | lift @0.30 |
|---|---|---|---|
| quiet (base <5%) | 13–15 | +3.8pp | +2.6pp |
| normal (5–12%) | 24 | +6.6pp | +6.0pp |
| active (>12%) | 21–26 | +13.4pp | **+15.9pp** |

Monotone. **The signal needs large moves to exist before it can find them.**
On 2026-08-25/26/27 the universe base rate was 2.6–3.6% and PCT7's lift was
roughly zero.

That is a conditional edge, not a broken one — but it means quiet tape produces
little, and any sizing should account for it rather than assuming a constant
edge.

## An error worth recording

The first scoring pass used threshold **0.5** and found 66 fires across only 14
dates, with 2026-07-29 alone contributing 25 of them and most of the hit rate.
That looked like cluster timing rather than stock selection, and the conclusion
drawn was to retire the model.

**That was wrong, and the cause was the threshold.** `scripts/monitor_pct7_ab.py`
already carried the right default — 0.20, *"since training base rate was 13%"* —
and at the intended cut the clustering vanishes: 65 dates, 189 tickers.

The operator overruled the retirement recommendation. Had it stood, the strongest
signal in the system would have been deleted on the basis of a badly chosen cut.

**The general lesson: a rare-firing signal evaluated at too high a threshold
looks like an artifact.** Check the intended operating point before concluding.

## What is NOT established

- **Sizing and capacity.** 1,105 selections over 65 dates is ~17 names a day.
  Position sizing, concentration limits and capacity are untouched.
- **Borrow and shortability** are irrelevant here (long-only signal) but
  liquidity of the selected names is not checked.
- **The model is fourteen weeks stale.** Retraining would reset the
  out-of-sample clock. The stale model's record is the benchmark any retrained
  version must beat — do not discard it.
- **Purged cross-validation** has not been run on PCT7's own training. The
  direction model was tested (leakage −0.0020, negligible) but PCT7 was not.
- **Regime conditionality is measured, not modelled.** No rule yet stands the
  signal down in quiet tape.

## Next steps, in order

1. **Set the operating threshold to 0.30** wherever PCT7 is read.
2. **Keep it in shadow mode** for now and let `analysis/pct7_status.py` accrue
   more dates — the cron runs Mondays at 11:00.
3. **Purged CV** on PCT7's training, matching what was done for the direction
   model.
4. **Only then** consider retraining, and benchmark against the frozen model's
   record rather than replacing it silently.
