## 5. Methodology rules — earned the hard way

**1. Equal-weight per date. Always.** Pooling stock-date rows has produced a fake
result **three times**: t = −20 on the SI brick (`Two_Brick_Findings` §5.1), and
+0.96% vs the correct −0.10% decile spread. Per-date first, aggregate second.

**2. Pre-register the cut.** Window, weighting, horizon, threshold — written down
*before* querying. One session read a single table four ways (*tails invert →
signal absent → sign flip by era → wide-universe positive*), each stated
confidently, each revised by the next query. That is slicing until something
looks significant.

**3. You cannot threshold your way to significance.** Demonstrated twice on
unrelated signals. Keeping the top 20% keeps ~20% of the signal and t falls ~√5.
A real signal shows up as **more dates at the same t**, never a higher t on fewer
names. (`prob_up` at 0.55/0.60/0.65/0.70 → 0 of 15; vanna at the top decile is
*weaker* than across the book.)

**4. A step completing is not a step working.** ~8 silent-failure defects this
week, **three in code written the same day to prevent that exact failure**: the
price seed ran but nothing checked it succeeded; metadata wrote to the wrong
column; the stale-feed scan excluded one of the ten feeds it existed to protect.
**Always assert on the result, not the exit code.**

**5. A control's SCOPE needs the same scrutiny as its logic.** `repair_stale_feeds`
ran correctly and reported success while excluding WCC — a metadata-only peer
that feeds peer-relative panels. Correct output, wrong coverage.

**6. Verify the number before building on the claim.** Four of 13 research
reports logged defects that **did not exist** (13F "stale", block-threshold
"malformed", GFS drift "false positive", NVDA 200-DMA "stale" — actually 0.4%
off, compared against the 50-DMA). All four share one cause: **a value rendered
without the context needed to read it.** A prior session built a fix for a "5%
error" that was 0.4%.

**7. Shadow books end on k, not on a calendar date.** Three months of momentum
shadow produced **k = 2** independent bets. A verdict was scheduled for Jun 29 for
a test that could not have enough data by Jun 29.

**8. A gate that scores FEATURES cannot see BOOK-level exposure.** Cross-
sectional IC and book beta are independent properties. 43 alphas passed
|t|>3 + mono + net-of-cost; all 43 built books with beta >= 0.87 at t 6-22.
The gate was measuring the axis that doesn't bind. Check construction before
adding another filter.

**9. A control's OUTPUT is not its COVERAGE.** `repair_stale_feeds` printed `not in universe or metadata (skipped): BNED, CYBR, IBM, JPM, RC` on every single run while reporting `stale=0`. Both were true — of the names in scope. `feed_freshness_check` passed simultaneously because it reads table-level `MAX(d)`. Two controls, one uncovered intersection, and the gap was printed in plain text for weeks. Read the qualifier lines, not just the verdict.

**10. Dedup on NAMES is not dedup on INFORMATION.** `alpha_select` grouped by
`alpha.split("__")[0]` -- a string prefix. All five deep-panel survivors reached
the gate through the same operator, `ts_argmax__w5`, which returns which of the
last 5 days held the max: a coarse 5-day momentum sign, identical whether the
series is RSI, %B or distance-from-52w-low. Pairwise Spearman 0.808-0.907; five
names, ~1.3 alphas. This is the third route past the dedup after base-name
collision and rank-equivalent transforms, and the only one where the VALUES
differ -- so rank-equivalence detection misses it. `--max-corr` culls on the
realized series over the selection window. Count the information, not the names.
